About Us ☕

Our Story of Coffee & Passion

From a small dream to a cozy corner loved by our community, Sleepy Bean is where every cup tells a story of passion, quality, and warmth.

Our Journey

From Bean to Cup

Sleepy Bean started with a simple idea: to create a place where people can slow down, enjoy a perfect cup of coffee, and feel at home. We carefully select our beans, roast them with love, and craft each drink to bring out the best flavors.

Whether you're here for a morning boost or an afternoon break, we're proud to be part of your day.
